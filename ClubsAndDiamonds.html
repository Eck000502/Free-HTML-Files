CLUBS & DIAMONDS

Tactical Rules

  • • MUST Follow Suit: If you hold a card matching the leading suit, you are forced to play it.
  • • Void Discards: If you are completely void of the leading suit, you may play ANY card (Clubs to trump, Diamonds to steal/points).
  • • Dominant Suit: The suit that leads is the highest suit (except Diamonds and Clubs).
  • • Clubs are always trump.
  • Scoring Conditions:
    • • Each Diamond = 1 Point
    • • Each Trick Won = 1 Point
    • • First to 50 points wins!
  • Sabotage Override: If 2+ Diamonds are played, they sabotage each other and are ignored. The trick drops to the highest Club. If no Clubs, the highest card of the Leading suit wins.
  • Tie-Breaker: Won by whoever captured the highest face value diamond in the last trick.
